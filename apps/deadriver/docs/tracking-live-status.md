## Verification and deployment

The integration build passed 301 tests and the 90-page Astro build. The final null-placeholder correction passed 44 relevant tests, including its new regression case. GHL forwards numeric/boolean values as text and missing optional fields as the string `null`; canonical normalization now prevents those transport differences from changing event identity or hiding a supplied reply snippet. One labeled QA-only duplicate activity exposed the null issue before correction; the retained QA history documents that test, and subsequent live replay uses the original activity. Contact duplicates and multiple opportunities per contact within a pipeline are disabled in the actual GHL settings.

The final catch-up encountered transient GHL indexing/network delays and a request timeout, then completed through idempotent replay. Independent read-only verification found zero missing facts, duplicate activity IDs, duplicated/missing notes, timestamp/campaign mismatches or contact-counter mismatches. The catch-up had no before-image preservation baseline; the earlier batch's preservation comparison remains separate evidence. One workspace blocklist fact was also retained without labeling it an unsubscribe. The combined real-campaign report excludes the draft QA campaign.

`/api/instantly-reconcile` is an authenticated recovery job on the existing Vercel Pro project, enabled every five minutes. It gives the primary GHL path at least five minutes before reconciling provider delivery history. It uses original event timestamps, the same immutable event identity, and completion receipts. Failed work remains eligible on the next run. It never sends emails or advertising conversions. This is the five-minute recovery mechanism; no extra paid GHL Wait/Webhook cycle was added. The automatic lookback is seven days; longer outages require an explicit historical recovery. The full GHL ledger search has a bounded pagination limit; a growing ledger requires monitoring for `recovery_incomplete` rather than silently truncating history. Production verification returned HTTP 200, five completed deliveries, zero unfinished/failed deliveries and zero conversions. Vercel's actual scheduled executions at 14:00:36 and 14:05:36 MDT also returned HTTP 200.

Live QA verified retained attribution across navigation, same-contact updates, saved-inquiry ordering, native CRM trigger authentication, SMS removal and test-record exclusion. Automated cases cover failed-form exclusion. Booking and watch-page visits did not fire Lead or Schedule. This does not claim an actual watch-video playback test.

## Where to look and maintain

Open the [GHL dashboard](https://app.gohighlevel.com/v2/location/dzfd13SYs0Jg3qbvmugD/dashboard/6ab67279ddc9aeb5d2a2fc2a). On a contact, use DRM Attribution & Outreach for original/latest campaign details and Outreach Activities for the dated history. Use Opportunities to select the channel's pipeline. Actual revenue comes from verified payments, not opportunity estimates.

The **DRM Attribution & Outreach** folder contains 71 distinct contact fields. Useful names include **DRM Original source**, **DRM Google GCLID**, **DRM Google GBRAID**, **DRM Google WBRAID**, **DRM Meta FBCLID**, **DRM Meta FBC**, **DRM Meta FBP**, **DRM Instantly original campaign name**, **DRM Instantly latest campaign name**, **DRM Instantly emails sent**, **DRM Instantly replies**, **DRM Instantly interested**, **DRM Instantly bounced**, **DRM Instantly unsubscribed**, **DRM Outreach appointments booked**, **DRM Outreach appointments attended**, **DRM Outreach customers won** and **DRM Outreach actual revenue by currency**. The [complete field-name/ID inventory](outreach-tracking-implementation.md#field-reference) retains all mappings; its earlier activation-status sections are historical.

Historical platform limitations: webhook delivery history cannot recreate events before subscriptions or outside retention. Lead-state snapshots provide cumulative open/click counts and observed statuses, not every dated transition. Missing individual events, reply bodies or sequence metadata are not invented. Snapshot totals and message events are reconciled rather than added blindly. Blocklist records are retained as suppression evidence without inventing an unsubscribe event. No custom lead labels existed at activation; support for a label does not mean such an event was received.

Keep field IDs, unique activity IDs, credentials and workflow authentication intact. Never copy secrets into notes or this repository. Check failed workflow executions and Vercel recovery logs before replaying an event; retries must retain original event/transaction IDs. Keep historical imports marked historical. New Instantly campaigns require their own All Events subscription and addition to the receiver's campaign allowlist. Keep the Search campaign paused until launch is authorized; the existing GHL premium execution costs were approved, but unrelated new paid services were not.
