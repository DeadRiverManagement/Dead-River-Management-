# Case-study search intent and copy — September 24, 2026

## Method

Reviewed current search results for agency case studies in each relevant industry and Google's primary search documentation. This is qualitative search-intent research, not a Keyword Planner, Search Console or DataForSEO volume export. No search volumes, keyword difficulty scores or ranking predictions are claimed. Client results come from the project record and Brandon's direct statements, not from competitor pages.

The audience is business owners evaluating marketing, growth or website help. Consumer searches such as “plumber near me” are not the target of these agency case studies. Terms appear naturally in unique titles, meta descriptions, industry labels, section headings and copy. No keyword meta tag, keyword stuffing, invented testimonials or unsupported client-specific SEO results were added.
