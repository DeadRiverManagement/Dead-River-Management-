## How much does a website cost for a small business?

Small business websites run $10 to $99 monthly on a DIY builder, $500 to $5,000 with a freelancer, and $5,000 to $30,000 with an agency.

Those ranges come from [Leadpages' website design price guide](https://leadpages.com/blog/average-cost-of-website-design-for-small-business), updated 13 July 2026. One caveat up front: Leadpages sells website software, so that is a vendor's read on the market, not neutral research. We name them rather than call it "industry data", because that is what it is.

Other 2026 guides land in the same territory from different angles:

| Route | What 2026 sources say it costs | Who published it |
|---|---|---|
| DIY builder (Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy, WordPress) | $10 to $99 per month | Leadpages, July 2026 |
| Freelance designer | $500 to $5,000 one time | Leadpages, July 2026 |
| Agency build | $5,000 to $30,000 and up | Leadpages, July 2026 |
| Template site: freelancer vs boutique agency | $3,000 to $8,000 vs $8,000 to $15,000 | [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/website-development-cost-2026-complete-pricing-data), April 2026 |
| Professional design service | $499 and up, or $640 and up for a small business site | [GoDaddy](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/skills/how-much-does-a-website-cost), February 2026 |

Digital Applied is a development agency and GoDaddy sells domains and hosting, so all three have a horse in this race. Read them that way. The spread is real and it is not a scam: it is who writes the words, who takes the photos, whether the lead form sends anywhere, and who fixes it in March when it breaks.

## Is $1,000 a good price for a website?

A $1,000 website is fair value if the price covers hosting, a mobile layout, working lead forms, and someone to fix things later.

At $1,000 you are at the bottom of the freelance range. Good deal or bad one, the difference is scope, not the price. Get four answers in writing first:

1. **Is hosting included?** Shared hosting is $3 to $10 a month on Leadpages' 2026 figures, so somebody pays it.
2. **Who writes the copy?** Leadpages puts copywriting at $50 to $150 a page. If you send the words, that is your weekend.
3. **What happens after launch?** A one time build with no maintenance means the next update is a new quote.
4. **Does the phone number tap to call?** Most customers only ever see the mobile version.

Cover all four and $1,000 is a bargain. Five pages of stock photos with no lead form is $1,000 you spend again next year.

## Is it cheaper to build the website yourself?

Building the site yourself is cheaper in cash and costlier in hours. A builder subscription runs $10 to $99 a month, plus your weekends.

Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy and WordPress all get a working site online. [Wix's own plans page](https://www.wix.com/plans) listed Light at $17.77 a month, Core at $29.77 and Business at $39.77 in September 2026, billed annually. GoDaddy's February 2026 guide puts builder plans at $9.99 to $20.99 and up per month.

What no subscription includes is everything that turns a site into booked jobs:

- **A domain**, $10 to $20 a year, and an **SSL certificate** if the plan does not bundle one, $10 to $100 a year (Leadpages, 2026).
- **Words.** $50 to $150 a page if you hire it out.
- **Photos.** $500 to $2,000 for photography and graphics. Your phone camera is free and beats stock.
- **The parts that produce leads.** Click to call, a form that emails you in seconds, a page per service, and page speed.

If you have the time, build it. Plenty of owners do. Just count the hours honestly.

## How much does a website cost per month and per year?

Website running costs are domain, hosting, SSL and maintenance. Add the published 2026 ranges and the total lands between $5 and $220 a month.

Here is the arithmetic, on Leadpages' July 2026 numbers:

| Line item | Low | High |
|---|---|---|
| Domain and privacy | $20/yr | $35/yr |
| Shared hosting | $3/mo | $10/mo |
| SSL certificate | $10/yr | $100/yr |
| Maintenance | $0/mo on a builder | $200/mo on a custom site |
| **Roughly per month** | **about $5** | **about $220** |
| **Roughly per year** | **about $60** | **about $2,640** |

Hosting and the domain are the cheap part. Maintenance decides your annual bill, and it is the line nobody mentions on the sales call.

## How much does it cost to maintain a website?

Website maintenance costs $0 to $50 a month on a builder and $50 to $200 a month on a custom site in 2026.

Those are Leadpages' figures, and GoDaddy's February 2026 guide puts ongoing maintenance at up to $500 or more a year, inside the same range. Maintenance means software and plugin updates, security patches, backups, keeping the SSL current, and checking the lead form still delivers. The last one matters most. We have opened small business sites where the contact form had been failing for months and the owner assumed the phone was quiet.

## How much does a one-page website cost?

A one-page website costs $500 to $5,000 built professionally, or the price of a builder subscription if you assemble it yourself over a weekend.

That $500 to $5,000 is GoDaddy's own answer, published February 2026. It is wide because "one page" means two things: a single scroll with your services and a phone number, or a conversion page with custom copy, photography and a booking form, which is most of a full site on one URL.

One page is often enough to start. It stops being enough once you want to be found for separate services or separate towns, because Google needs a page for each.

## So what should you actually pay?

Pick a DIY builder if you have time, a freelancer if you want the files outright, and a monthly service if you want it handled.

- **DIY builder.** Cheapest in dollars. You are the designer, the copywriter and the support desk.
- **Freelancer or agency.** You own it. Budget $500 to $30,000, then budget again for maintenance.
- **Monthly service.** Nothing upfront, someone else owns the upkeep, and you rent rather than buy.

## Common questions

### Who owns the website if you pay monthly?

With a $0 build and a monthly fee, you are subscribing to a site we build, host and maintain, not buying files outright.

Ask any monthly provider, us included, what happens to the site and the domain if you stop paying.

### Does a cheap website hurt your Google rankings?

Price does not affect rankings. Page speed, a working mobile layout, real text a search engine can read, and consistent business details do.

A tidy $97 a month site with clear service pages beats a $15,000 site built as one giant image. Our [local SEO page](/local-seo-el-paso) covers the rest.

### What does a local service business website actually need?

A local service website needs click to call, one working lead form, a page per service, your service area in text, and fast loading.

Sliders and video headers do not book jobs. A phone number a thumb can tap does.

### How much do the words and photos cost?

Copywriting runs $50 to $150 per page and photography or graphics runs $500 to $2,000, according to Leadpages' 2026 pricing guide.

That is the line most quotes leave out. Ask whether copy is included, and if not, how many pages you write.
