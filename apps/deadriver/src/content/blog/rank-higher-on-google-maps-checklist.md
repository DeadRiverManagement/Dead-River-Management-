Most of your calls come from [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps). That little map with three businesses on it. If you're not in it, you're not getting the call. This is the full list of what we do to get a client in there.

![Google Maps search results for a local service](/images/tools/google-maps.webp)

## Start with the profile

Optimizing a [Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/business/) takes an hour or two. Everyone can do it. So it isn't enough on its own. But you have to do it first.

![Google Business Profile sign-in page](/images/tools/google-business-profile.webp)

1. Nail your primary category. It's the biggest ranking factor there is. A roofer should be "Roofing contractor," not "Contractor."
2. Add every service you offer. Click into each one and write a description. Put your city and your main keyword in it.
3. Fill out every attribute and section. Hours, photos, the whole thing. Leave nothing blank.
4. Fill out the service area section. Add every town you actually work in.
5. Get new reviews every week. Consistency matters more than a big burst. Respond to every single one.
6. Post often. Each post should mention one city you want to show up in. One city per post, not five.

## Make your name, address, and phone match everywhere

This one gets out of hand fast. Google trusts your info more when it is the same on every site. That means exact. "St" and "Street" are not the same to Google.

1. Open your Google Business Profile. Copy your name, address, and phone number exactly as written.
2. Go to your website footer. Paste all three in, word for word.
3. Google your business name in quotes. Click every site that mentions you.
4. If any of them have your name, address, or phone wrong, fix it.

## Get citations

A citation is a listing of your business on another site. [Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com/), the Chamber of Commerce, places like that. Each one shows your name, address, and phone.

![Yellow Pages home page](/images/tools/yellow-pages.webp)

Google sees those three things repeated across the internet. The more they match, the more Google trusts you. We use [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/) for this. Yext does the same job. These tools also let you fix wrong info in one place.

![BrightLocal home page](/images/tools/brightlocal.webp)

## Put your website to work

Here's the thing most owners miss. Your website affects your Maps rank. You can't optimize the profile once and hope for first place. The profile is the easy part. Your website sends signals back to it.

### Embed your map on the contact page

1. Go to Google Maps and search your business.
2. Click Share, then Embed a map. Copy the HTML.
3. Open your website's contact page. Paste the code at the bottom.

This tells Google your website and your profile point to the same place. It takes two minutes.

### One page per city

You can't guess that Google knows you work in Socorro. You have to tell it. Build a real page on your website for each city you serve. Make each one unique. Pick one service per page. A roofer would build Roof Repair Horizon City. A plumber would build Water Heater Repair Socorro.

Stay realistic. A huge city or one far away is a long shot. Nearby towns usually work if you do it right. The best way to rank in another city is a second real location. That means its own profile. Most of us know that already.

## See the hidden data

Your profile hides most of its numbers. [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com/), the free tool that tracks your website visits, can show them.

![Google Analytics overview page](/images/tools/google-analytics.webp)

1. Open Google Analytics. Click Admin at the bottom left.
2. Scroll down to Product links. Click Google Business Profile links.
3. Click Link. Choose your listing, select your profile, and submit.
4. Go to Reports. You'll see a new Business Profile tab.

Now you get real data from your profile that is normally hidden.

## A note on Business Home

Google has a new dashboard called Business Home. It puts your Business Profile, your ads, YouTube, and Merchant Center in one place. It uses AI to update hours, answer reviews, and write posts. It also looks at everything together and tells you what to do next.

It's invite only right now. To qualify, you need to be based in the US. You need a verified profile with under 10 locations. And you can only run one type of business in it. So get verified and keep your accounts clean. That's how you get in when it opens up.

## Two cautions

First, never delete a profile you are unsure about. We had a client open a new location. They made a duplicate of their original profile by accident. Google flagged it and deleted it. Except it was their main profile, the one all their business came from. They were about to go under. They resubmitted it and it got suspended right away. So we put together a long appeal. Honestly, I was nervous. It came back with one review. Two hours later, every review showed up again. I couldn't believe it. So don't click delete until you know for sure.

Second, competitors sometimes report profiles to get them suspended. It's a real thing, and it works. Google takes the profile down first and asks questions later. So keep everything clean. Real address, real reviews, real info. Do things the right way and you win in the end.
