**What should an El Paso home service owner look at before picking a marketing team?**

A marketing team is a company you pay to bring you customers. Pick the team that helps you get found. Pick the one that helps you answer the phone. Pick the one that helps you **book the job**. Do not pick the team that only sells clicks. A click is when someone taps your ad. A click is not a job.

Dead River Management is based in El Paso. We offer **Demand Flow** to accepted businesses nationwide. Accepted means we talked first and agreed it fits. Strategic Key Marketing sells a custom “customer acquisition system” after a strategy session. A strategy session is a long sales call. The system is their plan to get you customers. They do **not** publish plan prices.

If you want booked jobs, compare five things. One, booked-job tracking. That means counting real jobs, not clicks. Two, missed-call coverage. That means someone answers when you can’t. Three, a written rule for any lead promise. A lead is a person who asks for a quote. Four, clear monthly fees. Five, local proof.
