You run a home service business in El Paso. You have been about the same size for a while. This post is about why that happens, and what we see change it.

## Progress tracks risk

I talk to a lot of owners. The ones who grow decide fast. They make the call. They accept it might not work. If it does not, they fix it quick.

The stuck ones sit on the decision. They wait for someone else to make the choice. They want it to feel magic. It is not.

Your progress is tied to the risk you can stomach. That is really it.

## Nothing changed, so nothing changes

Owners tell me they have been stuck for years. Then they tell me they have changed nothing. I do not get how you can say both out loud.

If it is not working, something has to change. You pick what. But something.

We are wired to see the downside more than the upside. It is usually the other way around. The downside is smaller than you fear. The upside is bigger than you think.

## You can be right, or you can win

Pick one.

Say you hire a marketing company. You can make a bad one work if you are committed. You can make a great one even better. But maybe you are committed to being right that it does not work. Then you will win that. Every time. No matter how good the agency is.

Same thing with ads. Same thing with SEO, which means showing up on Google. People say it does not work. It works for a huge number of businesses. The question is whether you are committed to making it work.

If you care more about being right than getting what you want, nothing will change.

## The trade

You cannot get to seven figures and keep everything else the same. There is always a trade.

Early on, the trade is time. Long days.

Later there is a second risk nobody warns you about. When you quit your job, the only thing you could lose was the job. Now you have a business to lose. And the hardest part of the next step is giving up control.

You built the process. You show up to every lead. You do the final walk through. Nobody will do it as well as you. Handing that to someone else feels like a risk. It is one. But you cannot grow past yourself without it. You are handing your expectations to another person. That is the job.

## Owners know the constraint. They avoid it.

Most owners know exactly what is holding them back. They just do not want to touch it.

They fall in love with the part they are good at. They build a quiet dislike for the part that needs fixing. Hiring is the usual one. They want to keep the same crew forever. They call them family. So they grind harder on the part that already works, and the constraint never moves.

That is how a business stays stuck for years. The owner works harder than ever. Nothing moves.

## The gutter cleaning call

Here is a small example. A gutter company gets a call for a gutter cleaning. The owner wants installs, not cleanings. So he treats that call as a distraction.

It is not a distraction. It is a lead. Someone called you. They already want help. That is the easiest sale of your week.

We see owners who have not answered a lead in two weeks. Before they even know what the lead is, they have an excuse. Those leads are no good. We are busy right now. That is not our ideal customer.

Home service businesses need an owner operator mentality. Someone who treats every call like it is their money. Because it is.

## A simple test for any new idea

New tools show up every week. Here is how we decide.

Picture a new team member who knows very little. Would this tool help them do the job well? If yes, we bring it in. If not, we skip it.

That is the whole test.

Two more quick ones.

- Do not build your own software. A CRM is the system that tracks your leads. The best one costs a few hundred a month. That is not the thing to cut. You run a roofing business. You do not run a CRM.
- When hiring, watch for one line. The line is "I am just looking for the best fit". To me that says they do not know what you do. And they do not much care. We try to make people feel special in an interview. They should try a little too.

## Not wanting to grow

Once in a while an owner is honest. "I do not want to grow. I just need a few more leads." I have real respect for that. It is rare.

Most people say they want to grow but will not do the trade. They want it but will not invest. That person knows where they want to be. That is a clear goal, and we can help with it.
