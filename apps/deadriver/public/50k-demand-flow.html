Demand Intelligence

Start With People Already Looking To Buy

People search, read and compare before they buy. Demand Intelligence uses that activity, called purchase intent data, to identify people looking for what you sell.

  • Find the demand: identify people actively researching your product or service.
  • Keep it fresh: use purchase signals from the last 24 to 48 hours.
  • Reach those buyers: focus your ads and email on an audience already showing interest.
  • Connect the next steps: Demand Flow turns that audience into inquiries, bookings and paying customers.
